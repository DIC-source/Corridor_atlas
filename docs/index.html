How to read these tables

Distances run from the lesion surface to the nearest atlas voxel, whether or not that layer is shown. “@” is depth from the entry point. critical marks eloquent cortex, motor, sensory, language and visual pathways, and arteries. Scores and distances describe the population atlas, not this patient.

All layers live in MNI ICBM152 2009 space at 1 mm. Both panels draw from the same label volumes, so a colour block on the MRI and the matching shape in the illustration are the same voxels.

How to use

The mouse wheel pages through slices; Ctrl + wheel (or pinch) zooms the atlas plate and MRI, right-drag or Shift-drag pans, and double-click fits. Click any structure, tract or artery in either view to open its details at the top of this panel. Click the MRI to move the crosshair; scroll or use the slider to page slices. Click a structure in the illustration to jump the MRI there. Use the Lesion tab to drop a sphere and list everything within 5 or 10 mm; use the Corridor tab to set an entry and target and see what the trajectory crosses.

Limits

Atlas tracts are group averages and do not show displacement, infiltration or oedema. Brainstem level boundaries are approximate axial planes, the STN is an ellipsoid at the published DISTAL-atlas centroid (±12, −13, −5.5) because the ATAG STN label is only a few voxels at 1 mm, and the substantia nigra is the ATAG label grown by 1 mm. CerebrA's “ventral diencephalon” label also covers the cerebral peduncles and midbrain tegmentum; it is split at the plane from the posterior commissure to the mammillary bodies, so only the part in front of that plane is called hypothalamus / subthalamic region, the peduncle (where corticofugal fibres run) is labelled separately, and the rest joins the midbrain. The arteries and the MRA image are population averages (Mouches & Forkert 2019), already in MNI space, covering the whole brain. Averaging across people keeps the large, consistent arteries sharp (ICA, circle of Willis, M1, A1–A2, pericallosal, P1–P2, basilar) but blurs distal branches whose course varies between people, so small cortical branches look faint or missing. The 3D vessel surface is drawn where at least 12% of subjects had a vessel. Branches are grouped by position: vessels outside the brain envelope (skull base, extracranial) form their own group, a box around the skull base forms the ICA and circle-of-Willis group, and the rest take the territory they run through. These grouped surfaces are off by default. All named vessels are generic: arteries are centerlines traced on the population vessel-probability atlas, small branches and veins are textbook courses, and every branch is joined to its parent vessel. Individual variants are deliberately left out. Named arteries (ICA, M1, M2, A1, A2–pericallosal, PComm, P1–P2, P3–P4, SCA, basilar, vertebral) are centerlines traced with a minimal-cost path along the highest vessel probability between anatomical landmarks, with calibre from the radius atlas. Veins and dural sinuses (superior and inferior sagittal, straight, transverse and sigmoid sinuses, vein of Galen, internal cerebral veins, basal veins of Rosenthal) are not in any reachable dataset: their courses are placed from the template's own geometry (inner brain surface, corpus callosum, third ventricle, midbrain) and are approximate. With “Cut at slice” on, the 3D model is cut at the current MRI level.

Planning mode

The Lesion & corridor tab is a separate planning mode; the lesion and corridor are hidden everywhere else. The lesion is the target. Probe’s-eye and inline views follow the layout of commercial trajectory planners. All scores describe a population atlas, not a patient.

How the routes are ranked

Place the lesion and every candidate route is scored and listed, lowest score first; the top one is applied automatically unless you untick “Pick the best-ranked route automatically”.

  • Which routes are candidates. A standard approach is listed only when the lesion lies within 15–30 mm of the approach’s usual target (further along the brainstem). A direct transcortical route (the best of the surface entries within 100 mm) is offered for lesions in the brain or on the convexity, never through the skull base, a cistern or the brainstem: its entry must be on the outer cortical surface with the scalp close by. Brainstem and deep lesions also get the two-point line [5].
  • Order. Total = trajectory risk score (below) + fit penalty (how far the lesion is from the approach’s usual target) + approach cost (what the approach itself sacrifices, e.g. hearing in translabyrinthine). The lesion’s location label (lobe, deep, infratentorial, brainstem) and its distance to the ventricle decide which approaches are eligible.
  • Natural corridors. Approaches through the Sylvian fissure, cisterns or skull base skip the sulcus, entry-cortex, angle and brain-traversed checks, and their vessel cost is capped, because the straight line only gives the direction: tissue and vessels along it are retracted or worked around.
  • Why / why not. Each row explains what the approach is for, why it fits this lesion, what it risks and how it compares with the routes ranked next to it.

Not validated: the factors come from published planners [1–4], but the weights, thresholds, approach costs and bands are this project’s own. Check the patient’s own imaging.

風險分數怎麼算、參考什麼

這是教學用的啟發式分數（heuristic score）：每個因子的計分項目取自已發表的自動化路徑規劃系統，但權重、門檻和 low／moderate／high 分界（<2、2–4、≥4）是本工具自訂，沒有經過臨床驗證。分數越低越安全。

Vessel clearance最高 3
沿路徑每 0.5 mm 取樣，量到最近血管的距離扣掉通道半徑；≥3 mm 計 0，越近越高，碰到血管直接標紅。血管層是本 atlas 的族群血管（具名＋示意＋Mouches 機率 ≥25%），不是病人的血管
依據：EpiNav（SEEG）安全邊界 3 mm [1]；Liu 2014（DBS）<3 mm 罰分、穿過血管＝最大成本 [3]
Sulcal crossing最高 1.5
腦表 30 mm 內 T1 暗區（腦溝 CSF，不含腦室）距離 <4 mm 開始計分
依據：EpiNav 避開腦溝 [1]；Marcus 2019 以腦溝代替血管當主要避開結構，距腦溝 ≥10 mm 才算 0 風險 [2]；Liu 2014 罰穿過溝底的路徑（血管走在溝裡）[3]
Ventricle最高 1.5
穿過腦室就計分；經腦室入路（Kocher、Keen、Frazier、transcortical、transcallosal）不計
依據：Liu 2014：離腦室 <2 mm 為硬限制（DBS）[3]
Eloquent entry最高 2
入口前 4 mm 落在 M1、S1、旁中央小葉、Broca、Wernicke、supramarginal、Heschl、V1
依據：一般神外原則（皮質切開避開功能區）；清單為本工具選定
Critical tracts最高 3
10 條族群 tract（HCP-1065：CST、corticobulbar、AF、SLF、IFOF、視放射、ML、FAT、UF、ILF）；每條計 1.5 × sin(路徑與纖維夾角)：垂直穿過最差，平行（parafascicular）最輕
依據：沿纖維走向、不橫切纖維的原則；角度計分是本工具自訂
Deep nuclei最高 2
穿過基底核、視丘、腦幹或腦神經核就計分
依據：避開深部灰質的一般神外原則；清單與權重為本工具自訂（Liu 2014 的 thalamus 項是 DBS 專用設計，不直接套用）
Length最高 1
70 mm 以下計 0，100 mm 計滿；>100 mm 標警告
依據：Marcus 2019（腦切片規劃）上限 100 mm [2]；EpiNav（SEEG）用 90 mm [1]
Entry angle最高 1
路徑與腦表法線夾角，20° 以下計 0，40° 計滿；>30° 標警告
依據：Marcus 2019 與法線夾角 <30° [2]；EpiNav（SEEG）用 25° [1]（本工具量的是腦表法線，不是顱骨）
Brain traversed最高 2
非自然通道的路徑：病灶前穿過的腦實質長度（不含腦室），10 mm 以內不計，50 mm 計滿。
依據：「減少腦組織穿越」的一般神外原則（transgression）；門檻為本工具自訂。
Approach cost0–1.2
入路本身的代價，與路徑無關：translabyrinthine +1.2（犧牲聽力）、transvermian +0.6（小腦性緘默）、presigmoid／Kawase／經鼻斜坡 +0.5、其他擴大入路 +0.3–0.4。只用在排序。
依據：教科書描述的入路相關併發症；數值為本工具自訂。

走自然通道（Sylvian 裂、腦池、顱底）的入路：sulcus、eloquent entry、angle、brain traversed 四項不計，血管分數上限 1.5，因為直線只代表方向，沿途組織與血管實際上是被撥開或繞過的。

Target、entry 和距離怎麼決定
  • Target＝lesion 中心。你放的病灶球心就是終點；取樣到病灶表面停止。選 approach 並勾「Also move the lesion to this approach's usual target」時，改用該入路常見目標（例如 Kocher→Monro 孔、pterional→ICA 分叉）。
  • 手動 entry：在 MRI／plate 點一下，程式從 lesion 往你點的方向射線，停在最外層腦表面當入口。
  • Approach 預設：入口和目標點是依教科書定位（例如 Kocher 為冠狀縫前 1 cm、中線旁 2.5–3 cm）手動放在 MNI 模板上的近似點；經皮質入路會再貼到腦表面。
  • Suggest safest entries：腦表每約 3 mm 一個候選點（z > −35，排除顱底），先篩掉長度 >100 mm、角度 >30°，選了 approach 時只留入口 25 mm 內的點，再用上面的分數排序，取彼此相距 ≥12 mm 的前 5 名（同 Marcus 2019：列出 5 條最低風險路徑讓醫師挑 [2]）。
  • Two-point direction：Brown、Thompson、Spetzler 的 two-point method [5]：lesion 中心連到最靠近的軟腦膜或室管膜表面，延長到皮質就是入口。原法是為腦幹病灶設計的。
  • 距離：近病灶表格從病灶表面量到最近的 atlas voxel，不論該圖層有沒有開；「@」是離入口的深度。critical 標示功能區皮質、運動／感覺／語言／視覺路徑和動脈。
  • 腦外段：入口之後沿同一直線延伸到頭皮（模板 T1 訊號降到背景處），所以顯示的是皮膚到病灶的完整路徑。

與已驗證系統的差別：EpiNav、Marcus 2019 和 Liu 2014 用的是病人自己的 CTA／MRV／顯影 MRI 血管與腦溝分割，而且是針對 SEEG、腦切片、DBS 這類細針路徑設計的；這裡是族群 atlas 加上開顱通道的直線近似。

Spine approaches: names, trajectories and ranking
  • Spine plate and CT. The cross-section is cut from BodyParts3D (DBCLS, CC BY-SA 2.1 JP), the same generic reference body as the vertebrae: back and neck muscles, psoas and abdominal wall, great vessels, viscera and skin, coloured as in cross-sectional atlases (muscle red-brown, fat pale yellow, cancellous bone inside a dark cortex). The model has no pharynx, larynx or lungs, and its carotid and jugular stop at C7–T1, so above that level the carotid sheath, the pharynx and larynx, and the lung (the rib-cage interior) are schematic. The canal is drawn from the measured canal size (epidural fat around the dural sac). The CT is a soft-tissue-window rendering of the same model, not a scan. Click any structure on the plate, MRI or CT to see its name.
  • Names. Spine approaches are named by the procedure, and the name carries the corridor, as in the literature. Interbody fusions are named by corridor: PLIF, TLIF, MI-TLIF, OLIF/ATP, LLIF and ALIF [8]. Endoscopic procedures are named by approach corridor + “endoscopic” + segment + procedure, e.g. TELD, IELD, PECF, LE-ULBD [9]. Each card lists the corridor, the visualisation (open, tubular, endoscopic, percutaneous) and the invasiveness components.
  • Invasiveness. The six components of the surgical invasiveness index [10]: anterior decompression (AD), fusion (AF) and instrumentation (AI), and posterior decompression (PD), fusion (PF) and instrumentation (PI), counted per vertebra. For a one-level operation this tool counts decompression once and fusion or instrumentation once per vertebra spanned. Interbody fusion through a posterior approach is counted with the posterior components. Each point adds 0.06 to the ranking, so the less invasive procedure wins a tie.
  • Trajectories. Each approach is its textbook line laid on one reference skeleton (BodyParts3D), using landmarks measured on it: pedicle and lateral-mass centres, canal, disc front and back, spinous tips. Screws use the published convergence and cranial angles (for example lumbar pedicle 8–20° medial, lateral mass 30° lateral and 15° up, C2 pedicle 25° medial and 25° up) and stop 4 mm short of the front of the body.
  • Candidates. An approach is listed only for the problems and levels it is used for. Its fit (0 = textbook first choice, up to about 1 = possible but unusual, 2.5 = abandoned) and approach cost (thoracotomy, oropharyngeal wound, an unusual side) are this tool’s own teaching values.
  • Risk. The clearance from the corridor or screw surface to every modelled structure within 8 mm: cord and tracts (weight 3), vertebral artery, Adamkiewicz, aorta, IVC and iliac vessels (2.5), anterior and posterior spinal and radiculomedullary arteries (1.5), roots, ganglia and spinal nerves (1.5), azygos (1), sympathetic chain (0.7), segmental arteries (0.6), dura (0.6), veins (0.4), dorsal rami (0.2). Inside the corridor counts the full weight, then it falls off over about 2.5 mm. Structures the approach normally retracts (dura in a microdiscectomy, the C2 ganglion in Goel–Harms) count a quarter. The worst structure of each kind counts fully, others a third. Total = risk + fit + cost; the bands (<2, 2–4, ≥4) match the brain side.
  • Not modelled. Oesophagus, trachea, carotid sheath, recurrent laryngeal nerve, lung, pleura, ureter, peritoneum, the psoas and most of the lumbar plexus are not in the model. Every route lists them under “Not modelled here”.

Not validated: teaching values on a single reference skeleton. Patient CT (pedicle width, VA course), MRI and CT angiography decide the real route.
